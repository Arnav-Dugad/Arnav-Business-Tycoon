# Tuning log

Every balance change is a knob file in `tools/content/knobs/` applied with `python -I tools/content/tune.py . <file>`, followed by a simulator run. Times are **median real time** after install. "Active" plays 3×15 min per day (plus day-one check-ins at 1.5 h and 3.5 h); "idle" plays 2×3 min per day (plus day-one check-ins at 1 h and 4 h).

### Target changes
- I briefly widened the idle bands during t04 (Chapter 1 to 1.5–5 h). At t05 the observed idle times sat inside the **original** ECONOMY.md bands, so the original bands were restored. **Apart from the one change below, no target has been loosened.**
- `idle first_manager` was relaxed from 8–15 min to **5–15 min**. A first manager inside the first session is good for idle players (it is what lets the game earn while they are away).

### Known limits (expected at this content level)
- After Chapter 2, only three businesses exist, so growth saturates. Dead session minutes and stalls are high late in day 2. More businesses arrive in Phase 5 (and markets, property and luxury after that), which give the money somewhere to go.
- `risky_trader` and `heavy_spender` cannot be simulated until markets (Phase 6) and the store (Phase 14) exist.

### Phase 5 target change
- `first_conglomerate` was 3–4 d (active) / 5–7 d (idle) in ECONOMY.md, written before the chapter structure existed. The merger tree is now Chapter 4's story goal, and Chapter 4 begins around 12 h (active) / 36 h (idle), so the old band would mean a day or more with nothing to do. New bands: **18 h–2 d active, 2–4 d idle**. This moves the target to match the design; it is not meant to hide a miss.

### Known miss: Chapter 8 (deferred to Phase 11)
- Once all 18 businesses are bought (around day 3 for active play), median net worth grows only about 0.3 decades per day: 1e19.3 at day 3, 1e20.3 at day 5, 1e21.4 at day 10. Space (unlock ≈ $8.0e20) and the final goal ($4.0e21) are therefore out of reach in the 5–6 d / 8–10 d bands.
- Steeper or flatter ladders only move the problem (p5e vs p5f). The planned sinks and sources that keep the late game growing are not built yet: markets (Phase 6), real estate (7), luxury (8) and Dynasty prestige (10). **Re-tune Chapter 8 in Phase 11 with that content in place.**
- Dead session minutes: active 62.5%, idle 25.6% (target < 5%). Same cause.


Slot formula now: 3 base + 1 per chapter beyond the first (max 6) + 1 per 3 HQ levels (max 8) = 17 free slots before Dynasty perks.
