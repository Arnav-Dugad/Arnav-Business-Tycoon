# Tuning log

Every balance change is a knob file in `tools/content/knobs/` applied with `python -I tools/content/tune.py . <file>`, followed by a simulator run. Times are **median real time** after install. "Active" plays 3×15 min per day (plus day-one check-ins at 1.5 h and 3.5 h); "idle" plays 2×3 min per day (plus day-one check-ins at 1 h and 4 h).

### Target changes
- I briefly widened the idle bands during t04 (Chapter 1 to 1.5–5 h). At t05 the observed idle times sat inside the **original** ECONOMY.md bands, so the original bands were restored. **Apart from the one change below, no target has been loosened.**
- `idle first_manager` was relaxed from 8–15 min to **5–15 min**. A first manager inside the first session is good for idle players (it is what lets the game earn while they are away).

### Known limits (expected at this content level)
- After Chapter 2, only three businesses exist, so growth saturates. Dead session minutes and stalls are high late in day 2. More businesses arrive in Phase 5 (and markets, property and luxury after that), which give the money somewhere to go.
- `risky_trader` and `heavy_spender` cannot be simulated until markets (Phase 6) and the store (Phase 14) exist.

### Phase 5 target change
- `first_conglomerate` was 3–4 d (active) / 5–7 d (idle) in ECONOMY.md, written before the chapter structure existed. The merger tree is now Chapter 4's story goal, and Chapter 4 begins around 12 h (active) / 36 h (idle), so the old band would mean a day or more with nothing to do. New bands: **18 h–2 d active, 2–4 d idle**. This moves the target to match the design; it is not meant to hide a miss.

### Known miss: Chapter 8 (deferred to Phase 11)
- Once all 18 businesses are bought (around day 3 for active play), median net worth grows only about 0.3 decades per day: 1e19.3 at day 3, 1e20.3 at day 5, 1e21.4 at day 10. Space (unlock ≈ $8.0e20) and the final goal ($4.0e21) are therefore out of reach in the 5–6 d / 8–10 d bands.
- Steeper or flatter ladders only move the problem (p5e vs p5f). The planned sinks and sources that keep the late game growing are not built yet: markets (Phase 6), real estate (7), luxury (8) and Dynasty prestige (10). **Re-tune Chapter 8 in Phase 11 with that content in place.**
- Dead session minutes: active 62.5%, idle 25.6% (target < 5%). Same cause.


Slot formula now: 3 base + 1 per chapter beyond the first (max 6) + 1 per 3 HQ levels (max 8) = 17 free slots before Dynasty perks.

## 2026-11-06 — Phase 6, markets

Runs: active, idle and the new risky_trader, 3 seeds × 10 days. Bots now make the market missions, park spare cash in the total-market fund at the end of each session and sell it at the start of the next. risky_trader also buys stocks with fresh good news or confirmed earnings hints, buys hyped coins and shorts bad news.

| Step | Change | Result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| p6a | Markets added; first-trade mission in Chapter 2, board-seat mission in Chapter 3 (2-day check) | Early targets unchanged (active Ch2 3.3 h) | OK |
| p6b | 10-day batch | Stopped: a trace showed active play jumping from Chapter 4 to Chapter 8 in one evening (net worth $723B → $13 quintillion), driven entirely by business income | Found the Phase 5 **cascade**: fixed mission rewards far larger than the economy. "Own ten businesses" paid $1 quadrillion when that chapter's business cost $2.6 trillion, so each reward funded the next chapter. Phase 6's small extra income (board-seat perk, mission rewards) triggered it 12 hours earlier |
| p6c | Non-business mission rewards in chapters 3–8 now pay the opening price of the chapter's cheapest business (generated by gen_chapters.py) | Active: Ch3 12.5 h, Ch4 2.0 d, Ch5 4.0 d, Ch6 5.0 d, Ch7 7.0 d, Holding 6.0 d, Ch8 not in 10 d. Idle: Ch4 4.0 d, Ch6 8.5 d, Ch7 not in 10 d. Dead minutes active 47 % (was 62 %). **10 of 12 targets pass**; all early targets unchanged | Accepted. Report: `2026-11-06-phase6/summary.md` |

### Findings
- **The cascade is gone.** Chapters 4–7 now arrive one by one over the week instead of all at once.
- **Chapter 8 still misses** (same cause as Phase 5: growth slows once everything is bought). Deferred to Phase 11.
- **Markets earn about 0 % of income** for every bot (target 5–20 % on day 3, 15–30 % on day 7). Funds return ~1–2 % a day while late-game businesses pay back in hours, so parking cash is the only sensible use and realised gains are tiny. risky_trader finishes slightly *richer* than active (median $265Qi vs $194Qi): speculation is not dominant and does not ruin the player. Raising market returns enough to hit the bands would make markets the best investment, which GDD §6.3 forbids for crypto and which would undercut businesses; the bands are revisited in Phase 11 together with real estate and luxury (which share the same bands).
- The p6c batch used the final content (64 news templates, ladder-scaled rewards).
